Features of the house hunting system

About House Hunting System

Mission

The mission is to come up with a system that helps the users to locate nearby vacant houses thus saving them time, money and the energy they would have spent looking for homes.

Vision

Create solutions that solves more users' problems in the future

How to use the app ?

  1. Open the app
  2. Register or Login
  3. On the search bar, find the home you're looking for
  4. click "more" to get further details of the house
  5. The map icon helps you locate the house and estimate its distance from you
  6. Contact section provides the contact details of the home owner who you can easily contact or chat with within the application
